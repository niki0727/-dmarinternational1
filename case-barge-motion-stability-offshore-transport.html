Barge Motion and Stability Review for Offshore Transport

This anonymized offshore transport case demonstrates how motion and stability review sharpened weather-dependent decision quality.

Project type

Offshore transport campaign using barge and support spread for heavy structures.

Challenge

Motion uncertainty and stability margin concerns under forecast weather variability.

Scope

Review of motion assumptions, stability envelopes, ballast strategy, and operational limitations.

Review basis

Hydrodynamic assumptions, transit plan, weather criteria, and vessel operating limits.

Main risk addressed

Transit instability and weather-related delay risk.

Result

Revised operating limits and clearer go/no-go criteria improved decision quality and reduced exposure.
