Offshore Wind Marine Assurance

Our offshore wind marine assurance team supports developers, EPCIs, and marine contractors with independent review from port to offshore completion. We provide offshore wind marine warranty style support and practical execution challenge so campaign teams reduce delay exposure while maintaining safety and insurability under commercial pressure.

Talk to an expert See case studies

Where assurance adds value in offshore wind

Clients involve us before vessel commitment, before campaign mobilisation, and when package interfaces between foundation, TP, tower, and nacelle scopes become schedule critical.

Typical scopes

  • Offshore wind marine assurance review of T&I methods and readiness
  • Offshore wind marine warranty style challenge on vessel and spread suitability
  • Campaign hold-point checks for weather-window execution confidence

Deliverables

  • Integrated assurance comment matrix across packages
  • Readiness status by workfront and vessel spread
  • Decision-ready briefings for project leadership and assurance functions

Key risks reduced

  • Serial delay from interface gaps between packages
  • Offshore waiting caused by weak readiness evidence
  • Late-stage rework from untested operational assumptions

Relevant sectors

  • Fixed-bottom and floating wind campaigns
  • Port staging and quayside preparation
  • Transport and installation across European basins

Vessel and package interfaces

  • Developer / EPCI / marine contractor interface
  • Foundation package to turbine package interface
  • Port logistics versus offshore sequence interface

Standards and guidance considered

  • DNV and IEC-relevant offshore wind guidance
  • IMCA marine operations practices
  • Client and insurer assurance requirements

FAQs

Yes. We often support existing MWS and project teams with additional technical depth and execution focus.
Do you attend offshore?
Where required, we support field verification at critical operation stages.
