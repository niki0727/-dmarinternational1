Marine Civils & Nearshore Operations

Assurance support for marine civils and nearshore works where tidal windows, temporary works, and vessel interfaces can drive major delivery risk. We help teams align methods with real operating constraints before mobilisation.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

Why nearshore is not low risk

  • Conflicting constraints from weather, tide, and port access
  • High interface density across marine and civils packages
  • Compressed windows that magnify late-stage documentation gaps

Typical work scopes

  • Nearshore pull-ins, coastal structures, and temporary works
  • Barge and support-vessel operations in constrained waters
  • Method and risk-review support before critical windows

Key interfaces

  • Civils contractor and marine spread interface
  • Port and regulatory constraints versus operational plans
  • Engineering assumptions versus vessel capability in practice

Deliverables

  • Prioritised close-out list with practical actions
  • Readiness summary for go/no-go decisions
  • Clear statement of residual risks and controls

FAQs

Can you support both nearshore and offshore phases?
Yes. Scope can cover transition points and full campaign interfaces.
Do you review temporary works and lifting sequences?
Yes. Both are core risk areas in nearshore delivery.
Can you work with short weather windows?
Yes. We focus reviews on critical-path risks and decision gates first.
