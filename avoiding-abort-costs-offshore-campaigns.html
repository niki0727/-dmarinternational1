Avoiding Abort Costs in Offshore Campaigns

Abort events are usually caused by combined technical, procedural, and timing gaps rather than one isolated issue.

What changes in volatile markets

Teams under pressure often focus on immediate mobilisation while unresolved assumptions remain in procedures, rigging plans, or weather thresholds.

How to reduce execution risk

A structured assurance process can reduce abort probability by setting clear hold points and contingency logic before offshore execution starts.

Practical action for project teams

Build a campaign readout that links each hold point to evidence, owner, and close-out date, then test contingencies before go-live.
