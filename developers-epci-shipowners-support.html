Support for Developers, EPCIs & Shipowners

Independent marine assurance support tailored to stakeholder needs across planning, readiness, and execution. We help developers, EPCIs, and shipowners make better technical decisions under schedule pressure without losing control of delivery risk.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

How needs differ by stakeholder

  • Developers: governance visibility and campaign risk control
  • EPCIs: interface closure and readiness decisions on tight schedules
  • Shipowners: vessel-fit evidence and operational boundary clarity

Typical support scopes

  • Method and RAMS review for critical marine operations
  • Vessel suitability and package interface assurance
  • Execution-phase close-out support for high-risk actions

When to involve an independent consultant

  • Before vessel commitment and final contracting decisions
  • Before mobilisation where unresolved interfaces remain
  • When schedule compression increases rework and standby risk
