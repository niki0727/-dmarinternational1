Quayside Loadout Review for Heavy Module Mobilisation

This anonymized quayside mobilisation case shows how interface control and temporary-works clarity reduced execution friction.

Project type

Heavy module mobilisation from yard to marine transport spread.

Challenge

Tight quayside sequence with temporary works dependencies and multi-party handover risk.

Scope

Review of SPMT transfer sequence, rigging and temporary works assumptions, and quayside interface controls.

Review basis

Approved loadout procedures, yard data, temporary works documents, and readiness hold points.

Main risk addressed

Transfer disruption and damage risk from unclear responsibilities and unresolved temporary works issues.

Result

Clarified handover controls and close-out actions supported safer transfer and improved schedule reliability.
