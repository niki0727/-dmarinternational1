Renewables Marine Assurance

Marine assurance for offshore renewables campaigns, focused on transport and installation readiness across high-interface packages. We help reduce technical and execution risk from early planning through mobilisation and offshore delivery.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

Why renewables campaigns need assurance

  • Complex package interfaces across turbine, foundation, and cable scopes
  • High vessel demand and compressed installation schedules
  • Cost exposure from delays and aborted weather windows

Typical packages

  • Foundation, turbine, and offshore substation installation campaigns
  • Export and inter-array cable transport and installation interfaces
  • Port staging and offshore handover readiness activities

Common interfaces

  • Developer, EPCI, and marine contractor responsibilities
  • Engineering intent versus vessel and spread capability
  • Port logistics versus offshore sequence and weather logic

Deliverables

  • Practical action register and close-out support
  • Readiness summaries for key decision gates
  • Evidence pack for assurance and stakeholder alignment
