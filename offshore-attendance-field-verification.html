Offshore Attendance & Field Verification

On-site verification support for high-consequence offshore operations. We confirm that approved plans, constraints, and interfaces are being applied in real conditions.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

Why field verification matters

Execution pressure can drive deviation from approved methods. Independent attendance keeps close-out factual and timely.

Typical attendance scopes

  • Critical lifts and transfer stages
  • DP trials and readiness checkpoints
  • Transport and installation hold points

Common disconnects between plan and execution

  • Unclosed actions at mobilisation
  • Operational limits applied inconsistently
  • Assumptions that do not match offshore conditions

Reporting outputs

  • Field verification notes and action priorities
  • Daily/phase readiness updates
  • Clear evidence for project governance

FAQs

Does this replace marine supervision?
No. It is independent verification support.
Can you mobilise quickly?
Yes, for critical windows and hold points.
Can attendance include close-out tracking?
Yes. We can track actions through completion.
