Offshore Attendance and Field Verification

Our offshore attendance and field verification support gives project teams an independent set of eyes at critical operation stages. We verify execution against approved methods, highlight deviations early, and keep close-out practical when campaign windows are tight and vessel-day exposure is high.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

When clients involve us

Clients involve us during execution-critical phases, at high-risk hold points, and when independent in-field confirmation is needed for stakeholders and insurers.

What we review

  • Execution against approved procedures and RAMS controls
  • Hold-point readiness, contingency triggers, and stop criteria
  • Deviation logging, close-out status, and real-time risk escalations

Deliverables

  • Field verification reports with action priorities
  • Readiness updates for project and marine management teams
  • Clear evidence trail for assurance and governance decisions

Typical risks reduced

  • Procedure drift during time-pressured offshore operations
  • Unreported deviations that become late-stage failures
  • Repeat abort events from weak in-field close-out discipline

Relevant sectors

  • Offshore wind transport and installation scopes
  • Subsea cable and nearshore campaigns
  • Oil and gas and decommissioning marine operations

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Marine superintendent and vessel master interface
  • Offshore installation team and onshore engineering interface
  • Client / EPCI / contractor readiness decision interface

Standards and guidance considered

  • Approved project RAMS and execution procedures
  • IMCA-aligned marine operations practices
  • Client and insurer assurance expectations

FAQ

Do you replace vessel crew supervision?
No. We provide independent verification and reporting to strengthen oversight and decision confidence.
Can you mobilise on short notice?
Yes. We can mobilise for critical windows and prioritise high-risk operation stages.
Do you only support offshore attendance?
No. We can combine field attendance with pre-mobilisation desk review and close-out support.
