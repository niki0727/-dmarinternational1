Offshore Attendance and Field Verification

Field verification support during offshore execution to close the gap between procedures and real conditions.

Talk to an expert See case studies

Scope we review

  • Witnessing of hold points and execution-critical operation stages
  • Verification that approved method statements are followed in-field
  • Rapid reporting of deviations, close-out actions, and readiness status
  • Interfaces between engineering, marine spread, and offshore execution teams

Typical outputs

  • Independent comment matrix with clear close-out actions
  • Readiness status and hold-point recommendations
  • Go/no-go criteria linked to weather and vessel limitations
  • Concise reporting for developers, EPCIs, shipowners, and insurers

Accredited Marine Warranty Surveyors (SOMWS-qualified or equivalent) and experienced marine consultants to support EPCIs, developers, and MWS companies during high-risk offshore operations.
