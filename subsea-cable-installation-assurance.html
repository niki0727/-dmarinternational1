Subsea Cable Installation Assurance

Our cable installation assurance service gives project teams an experienced subsea cable marine consultant perspective before offshore windows open. We focus on readiness, interface clarity, and practical risk control for export and inter-array campaigns where nearshore complexity and vessel availability can quickly affect schedule performance.

Talk to an expert See case studies

When clients involve us

Clients involve us before cable vessel commitment, before landfall works, and when route, burial, or crossing assumptions need independent challenge.

What we review

  • Cable installation assurance for procedure and spread readiness
  • Subsea cable marine consultant review of pull-in, crossing, and nearshore interfaces
  • Readiness checks for weather limits, contingency logic, and campaign sequencing

Deliverables

  • Action-led technical review report
  • Close-out tracker across cable, marine, and civil interfaces
  • Readiness summary for project and assurance decision makers

Typical risks reduced

  • Rework from weak nearshore interface definition
  • Standby caused by unresolved vessel/process constraints
  • Abort risk where contingency logic is unclear

Relevant sectors

  • Export and inter-array cable campaigns
  • Nearshore landfalls and pull-ins
  • Crossing and burial operations

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Cable contractor and marine spread interface
  • Nearshore civils and offshore execution interface
  • Survey / engineering assumptions versus vessel capability

Standards and guidance considered

  • DNV/IMCA-aligned marine operation guidance
  • Client cable installation standards
  • Project-specific risk and assurance frameworks
