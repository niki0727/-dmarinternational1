How Assurance Supports Procurement Decisions During Volatility

Procurement decisions in volatile markets often lock in cost and risk long before execution starts. Independent assurance can improve those decisions.

What changes in volatile markets

When vessel demand tightens, teams may commit to options with hidden technical limitations simply to secure capacity.

How to reduce execution risk

Independent review of suitability, readiness, and method assumptions gives procurement teams decision evidence beyond headline day rates.

Practical action for project teams

Use assurance findings as part of bid evaluation criteria so commercial speed does not compromise execution reliability.
