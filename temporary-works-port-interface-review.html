Temporary Works and Port Interface Review

Our temporary works review and quayside marine assurance support helps teams control high-consequence interfaces before loadout, transfer, and sail-away. We focus on practical readiness evidence so port and yard execution can proceed with fewer surprises and lower delay exposure.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

When clients involve us

Clients involve us before major quayside transfers, during temporary works design updates, and when multi-party port interfaces increase schedule and safety risk.

What we review

  • Temporary works assumptions for loadouts, transfers, and heavy lifts
  • SPMT, crane, and vessel handover interface controls
  • Port logistics constraints, sequencing logic, and hold-point readiness

Deliverables

  • Action-oriented findings report and close-out tracker
  • Readiness summary for port, yard, and project leadership teams
  • Go/no-go support based on practical interface evidence

Typical risks reduced

  • Quayside delays from unclear transfer responsibilities
  • Rework caused by unresolved temporary works constraints
  • Campaign disruption from weak yard-to-marine handovers

Relevant sectors

  • Ports, shipyards, and heavy-lift staging campaigns
  • Offshore wind and project cargo preparation scopes
  • Oil and gas and decommissioning transfer operations

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Yard engineering and marine operations teams
  • SPMT, crane, and vessel interface decision chain
  • Port authority requirements and project execution constraints

Standards and guidance considered

  • Project temporary works and lifting standards
  • DNV/IMCA-relevant marine operations guidance
  • Client and insurer assurance criteria
