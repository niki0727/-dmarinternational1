Nacelle and Tower Transport Assurance for Offshore Wind

This anonymized offshore wind transport case demonstrates how targeted assurance protected cargo integrity under schedule pressure.

Project type

Offshore wind nacelle and tower transport campaign with high-value cargo.

Challenge

High commercial pressure to accelerate mobilisation while preserving transport integrity margins.

Scope

Sea-fastening and transport readiness review covering securing design, route assumptions, and operating limits.

Review basis

Transport plans, cargo data, vessel limitations, and weather-operability assumptions.

Main risk addressed

Cargo stability and securing failure risk during transit in variable conditions.

Result

Updated limits and close-out actions improved confidence for go/no-go decisions and reduced avoidable standby.
