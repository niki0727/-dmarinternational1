Decommissioning Marine Assurance

Independent assurance for offshore decommissioning campaigns, covering vessel selection, lift interfaces, removal transport, and readiness controls. We focus on practical risk reduction so high-consequence operations progress with clearer hold points and fewer late-stage surprises.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

Decommissioning operational risks

  • Lift and removal complexity under constrained weather windows
  • Interface failures between engineering assumptions and offshore execution
  • Transport and disposal delays from incomplete readiness controls

Typical scopes reviewed

  • Heavy-lift plans, sea-fastening strategy, and tow/transport logic
  • Vessel suitability and capability limits for removal packages
  • Method statements, risk controls, and contingency pathways

Timing of involvement

  • Before vessel commitment and major engineering freeze points
  • Before offshore mobilisation and critical hold-point decisions
  • During close-out of high-risk comments and readiness actions

Deliverables

  • Prioritised technical comment and action register
  • Readiness status with practical go/no-go criteria
  • Summary outputs for project, assurance, and insurance stakeholders
