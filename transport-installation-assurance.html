Transport & Installation Assurance

Our transport and installation assurance service gives project teams an independent offshore transport review before expensive mobilisation steps. We test technical assumptions, sequence logic, and interface controls so installation marine assurance is practical, defensible, and aligned with delivery schedules under real commercial pressure.

Talk to an expert See case studies

When clients involve us

Clients involve us before route lock, before sail-away, and before weather-critical installation windows where one missed assumption can drive major standby cost.

What we review

  • Offshore transport review of route, motions, and operational limits
  • Installation marine assurance of lifting, sequencing, and interfaces
  • Readiness checks for vessel spread, contingencies, and hold points

Deliverables

  • Integrated transport-installation comment matrix
  • Readiness and hold-point tracker
  • Executive summary for risk and schedule decisions

Typical risks reduced

  • Cargo damage or instability during transit
  • Installation delays from unresolved sequence conflicts
  • Cost overrun from avoidable offshore standby

Relevant sectors

  • Offshore wind components and foundations
  • Subsea assets and heavy marine structures
  • Oil and gas and decommissioning transport-install scopes

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Barge / HLV / installation vessel interface
  • Port and quayside handover interface
  • Engineering package versus offshore execution plan

Standards and guidance considered

  • DNV marine operation references
  • IMCA lifting and offshore operations guidance
  • Project-specific insurer and client requirements

FAQ

Can you support both transport and installation in one workstream?

We can start with critical documents first and expand to full package coverage in staged cycles.
Do you support contingency planning?
Yes. We include practical abort and recovery logic linked to weather and vessel constraints.
