When to Involve Us in a Project

Earlier independent review usually prevents costly rework and standby. This page explains the best entry points for marine assurance support across the project timeline.

Early-stage review

Use independent review during concept and planning to stress-test assumptions before they become contract constraints.

Pre-vessel commitment

Bring assurance in before vessel fixation to challenge capability fit, limitations, and interface dependencies.

Pre-mobilisation

Run focused close-out reviews before mobilisation to reduce late surprises and avoidable abort events.

Execution-stage support

Use attendance and rapid close-out support at critical hold points where timing pressure is highest.

FAQs

Is it too late if a campaign has already started?
No. Execution-stage review can still reduce loss and improve control.
What is the most cost-effective timing?
Typically before vessel commitment and before mobilisation.
Can review scope be phased?
Yes. We can start with highest-risk interfaces and expand.
