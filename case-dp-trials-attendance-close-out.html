DP Trials Attendance and Action Close-Out Support

This anonymized DP readiness case shows how focused attendance and close-out support improved execution confidence.

Project type

DP-critical offshore campaign requiring readiness confirmation after trials findings.

Challenge

Outstanding findings and limited time before execution increased uncertainty in station-keeping readiness.

Scope

DP assurance attendance and targeted close-out verification on critical findings and contingency responses.

Review basis

Trial results, DP procedures, capability statements, and campaign-specific operability criteria.

Main risk addressed

Station-keeping performance risk and schedule loss from unresolved DP actions.

Result

Prioritised close-out and independent verification supported safer readiness decisions before mobilisation.
