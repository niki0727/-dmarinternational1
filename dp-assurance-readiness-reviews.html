DP Assurance & Readiness Reviews

Our DP assurance consultant team supports offshore projects with independent DP capability review and DP readiness check services. We help delivery teams confirm station-keeping assumptions, close critical findings, and align procedures with real operating constraints before high-value offshore windows open.

Talk to an expert See case studies

When DP assurance is needed

Clients involve us before DP-critical campaigns, after trial findings, or when vessel changes introduce new readiness uncertainty.

Scope of review

  • DP assurance review of capability statements and limitations
  • DP capability review against operation-specific conditions
  • DP readiness check for procedures, contingencies, and action close-out

Deliverables

  • DP-focused findings register with priority actions
  • Readiness statement linked to campaign conditions
  • Decision support for go/no-go and contingency planning

Typical findings

  • Loss of schedule from unresolved DP limitations
  • Unsafe assumptions during station-keeping operations
  • Costly waiting on weather windows without verified readiness

Relevant sectors

  • Offshore wind heavy lift and installation
  • Subsea and field development work
  • Complex offshore construction and intervention scopes

Vessel and project interfaces

  • Vessel DP team and project marine management
  • DP limits versus lifting and construction method
  • Control room procedures versus offshore task demands

Standards and guidance considered

  • IMCA DP guidance and operating practices
  • Class/flag and project DP requirements
  • Client standards for DP-critical operations

FAQs

Is this only for DP3 vessels?
No. We support DP readiness across project-relevant classes where operational risk justifies independent review.
Can you support after incidents or near misses?
Yes. We can provide targeted assurance and readiness rebuilding focused on repeat-risk reduction.
Do you replace vessel assurance teams?
We complement internal teams with independent challenge and clearer decision evidence.
