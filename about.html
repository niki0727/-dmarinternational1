About DMAR International

Hands‑on experience at sea and across the offshore oil & gas, renewables and shipbuilding sectors. Involved at every stage: marine operations, project management, client representation and MWS consultancy.

Core expertise

  • Pipeline and cable laying; installation spreads and anchor patterns
  • On/Off‑hire, suitability surveys and inspections (Jack‑Up, Semi‑Sub, HLV/HCV, tugs, barges, flotels, floating cranes, crew boats)
  • Anchor handling, towing, jacket launching, topsides float‑over, hook‑up, barge moves and positioning
  • Cargo handling, ballast & stability control, tank cleaning and environmental considerations
  • Safety preparedness and training; lifting gear and deck plant maintenance

Project attendance

  • Sakarya: Deepwater pipeline installation (Phase I)
  • Zohr: Subsea development, Petrobel / Saipem
  • Vineyard Wind: Offshore wind foundations and cable installation
  • Kashagan I&T: AGIP / NCOC, D-Island integration and tow
  • BP ACG (Azeri-Chirag-Gunashli): Platform transport and load-out assurance
  • TCO FGP – Tengiz: Module transport and offload supervision
  • Borwin 3: Offshore converter platform, Drydocks World Dubai
  • Dalma Gas Development: ADNOC / NPCC, load-out and towage support
  • Bul Hanine: Qatar Petroleum / McDermott, platform transport assurance
  • Al Fadhil: Petrofac / Saudi Aramco, pipeline installation MWS review

Professional practice

At DMAR International, we operate in accordance with internationally recognized codes and best-practice frameworks to ensure the safety, quality, and reliability of all marine and offshore activities.

Our work is guided by the industry’s leading technical standards and reference documents, including the Joint Rig Committee, DNV-ST-N001, DNV-RP-0360, and IMCA guidelines.

Our professional framework also aligns with the Society of Offshore Marine Warranty Surveyors (SOMWS) Code of Conduct, ensuring every engagement is executed with independence, transparency, and the consistent application of internationally recognized best practices.

Accredited Marine Warranty Surveyors (SOMWS-qualified or equivalent) and experienced marine consultants to support EPCIs, developers, and MWS companies during high-risk offshore operations.
