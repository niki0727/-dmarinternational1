When to Involve Marine Assurance Before Vessel Commitment

Early assurance involvement can prevent expensive changes after vessel contracts are fixed.

What changes in volatile markets

Vessel commitments often happen before engineering assumptions are fully stress-tested. That creates hidden technical debt in campaign planning.

How to reduce execution risk

Pre-commitment review of RAMS, loads, weather logic, and vessel capability exposes constraints while options are still open.

Practical action for project teams

Bring assurance in before commercial lock-in, not only before sail-away, to avoid redesign loops and standby exposure.
