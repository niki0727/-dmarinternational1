Vessel Suitability Surveys for Offshore Operations

Our vessel suitability survey support helps offshore teams validate vessel capability before contract lock-in. We provide an independent vessel suitability assessment for offshore projects, checking technical fit, limits, and interfaces so procurement, engineering, and marine operations teams can commit with better confidence and fewer late changes.

Talk to an expert See case studies

When to commission one

Clients involve us before vessel nomination, during bid evaluation, or when execution assumptions have changed and suitability needs rapid re-check.

What a vessel suitability survey covers

  • Vessel suitability survey against project-specific mission profile
  • Vessel suitability assessment offshore including deck, crane, DP, and weather limits
  • Gap analysis between proposed vessel capability and operation method

Outputs and recommendations

  • Suitability report with critical findings ranked by impact
  • Action list for owners, charterers, and project teams
  • Decision-ready summary for commercial and technical stakeholders

Common issues identified

  • Selecting a vessel with hidden technical constraints
  • Standby cost from overlooked capability limitations
  • Schedule slip from late vessel substitution

Related sectors

  • Offshore wind installation and support campaigns
  • Subsea cable lay and nearshore work
  • Heavy transport and decommissioning scopes

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Owner / charterer / project controls interface
  • Vessel master and offshore superintendent interface
  • Engineering assumptions versus vessel operating envelope

Standards and guidance considered

  • DNV and class-related vessel capability guidance
  • IMCA references for operational readiness
  • Flag, class, and contractual acceptance criteria

FAQs

How early should a suitability survey happen?
Best results come pre-award, while vessel options remain open and technical risk can still influence contracting decisions.
Can you review multiple vessel options quickly?
Yes. We can run side-by-side comparison with critical risk flags so teams choose fit-for-purpose tonnage faster.
Do you attend onboard?
When required, we support onboard verification and close-out before mobilisation.
