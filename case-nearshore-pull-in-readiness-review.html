Nearshore Pull-In Readiness Review

This anonymized nearshore case outlines how early marine-civil alignment improved pull-in readiness in narrow weather windows.

Project type

Nearshore cable pull-in campaign with tight weather and permit windows.

Challenge

Cross-discipline interface gaps between marine spread and nearshore civils increased execution uncertainty.

Scope

Independent readiness review of pull-in method, contingency logic, and interface controls before mobilisation.

Review basis

Method statements, risk controls, nearshore constraints, and vessel-operability assumptions.

Main risk addressed

Abort and remobilisation risk from incomplete interface close-out and unclear contingency triggers.

Result

Critical interface actions were closed earlier, improving execution confidence in a narrow weather window.
