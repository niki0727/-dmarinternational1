Ports, Shipyards & Heavy-Lift Support

Our heavy lift marine consultant team supports yards and staging ports with SPMT loadout review and quayside marine assurance. We help clients align engineering intent with operational reality so transfers, lifts, and handovers are controlled before offshore schedule pressure amplifies interface risk.

Talk to an expert See case studies

When clients involve us

Clients involve us before complex loadouts, before major quayside transfers, and when multi-party yard interfaces need independent technical governance.

What we review

  • SPMT loadout review and transfer sequence checks
  • Quayside marine assurance for lifting, rigging, and temporary works
  • Heavy lift marine consultant review of hold points and contingencies

Deliverables

  • Structured comments and close-out register
  • Readiness evidence package for critical operations
  • Go/no-go briefing aligned to practical constraints

Typical risks reduced

  • Quayside incidents from interface misunderstanding
  • Transfer delay from unclosed temporary works issues
  • Offshore schedule impacts from weak yard readiness

Relevant sectors

  • Ports and fabrication yards
  • Offshore wind and heavy module projects
  • Project cargo and transport preparation scopes

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Yard engineering and marine team interface
  • SPMT / crane / vessel handover interface
  • Port authority and project operations interface

Standards and guidance considered

  • IMCA and DNV-relevant operational guidance
  • Project temporary works and lifting standards
  • Client and insurer acceptance criteria
