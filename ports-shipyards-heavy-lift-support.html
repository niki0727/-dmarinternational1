Ports, Shipyards & Heavy-Lift Support

Technical marine support for port and yard campaigns where loadout, transfer, and readiness interfaces must be controlled before offshore execution starts. We provide independent checks that reduce quayside delay risk and strengthen handover confidence.

Talk to an expertSee case studies

Typical project scenarios

  • SPMT loadouts and barge transfer preparation
  • Heavy module mobilisation in fabrication yards
  • Port staging and offshore handover interfaces

Quayside and berth risks

  • Transfer incidents from unclear operational boundaries
  • Berth constraints and metocean assumptions not reflected in plans
  • Campaign disruption from unresolved hold points

Temporary works and interfaces

  • Temporary support structure and rigging review
  • Interface checks between yard, marine spread, and port authority
  • Practical alignment of engineering intent to execution conditions

Deliverables

  • Action-focused review report and close-out register
  • Readiness evidence pack for decision-makers
  • Concise summary of residual risk and operating limits
