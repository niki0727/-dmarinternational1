Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2024

Who we are

DMAR International MB is a marine warranty and offshore consultancy headquartered at Litorinos g. 3, Kalotės km., Klaipėdos raj., 92275, Lithuania. We are responsible for the personal data we collect and use when you interact with us.

What data we collect

  • Contact enquiries: name, business email address, phone number (if supplied) and the details you include in your message.
  • Careers submissions: name, contact details, CV or covering information, location, role preferences and availability notes.
  • Operational correspondence: project documentation exchanged during active engagements.
  • Website usage: basic analytics data when you allow non-essential cookies.

How and why we use your data

We only use your information for clearly defined purposes:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide proposals (performance of a contract or steps prior to entering a contract).
  • To manage our talent network and resource projects (legitimate interest in building a qualified marine and offshore team).
  • To meet legal, regulatory or insurance obligations connected with marine warranty and offshore operations (legal obligation).
  • To improve our site and messaging when analytics cookies are enabled (consent).

Retention

Contact enquiries are retained for up to 24 months unless an engagement is created. CV submissions and associated notes are retained for up to 12 months so we can revisit opportunities and maintain an audit trail of our resourcing decisions. Engagement records are kept for the duration of the contract plus seven years to satisfy underwriter and statutory requirements.

Sharing and processors

We do not sell personal data. We share information only with trusted processors who support our operations, including secure email, document storage, and communication tools. These processors act on our instructions and are subject to contractual confidentiality and data processing agreements.

International transfers

Where data moves outside the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or an equivalent safeguard recognised under GDPR/UK GDPR. We ensure our processors provide adequate protection and transparency over where data is stored.

Security

We apply access controls, encryption for data in transit and at rest, and regular reviews of our systems and partners. Only authorised personnel and vetted contractors access personal data, and they do so under confidentiality agreements.

Your rights

You can request access to your data, ask for corrections, object to certain processing, request deletion, or ask us to transfer your information to another controller. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

Contact us

For privacy enquiries or to exercise your rights, email privacy@dmarinternational.com or write to DMAR International MB, Litorinos g. 3, Kalotės km., Klaipėdos raj., 92275, Lithuania.
