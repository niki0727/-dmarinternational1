Privacy Notice

Last updated: 05 March 2025. This notice explains how DMAR International MB collects and uses personal data and the choices available to you.

Who we are

DMAR International MB is a private limited company registered in Lithuania (company number MB 306044652). Our registered office is Litorinos g. 3, Kalotės k., Klaipėdos raj., 92275, Lithuania. We act as the data controller for the information described in this notice.

What data we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to provide or that is necessary to operate our services, including:

  • Identity and contact details (name, email address, phone number, job title, company) submitted through our contact forms.
  • Career information (CV/ résumé details, qualifications, location, availability) submitted via the careers upload form.
  • Operational details about projects or enquiries that you share with us to scope work.
  • Technical diagnostics from essential cookies required to keep the site secure and functioning.

How and why we use your data

We process personal data only where we have a lawful basis under UK GDPR, EU GDPR, and the Lithuanian Law on Legal Protection of Personal Data:

  • To respond to enquiries and provide proposals (legitimate interests — running our consultancy and responding to client requests).
  • To assess job applications and maintain our specialist network (consent when you upload a CV; legitimate interests in resourcing projects).
  • To meet legal or regulatory obligations such as sanctions screening or insurance requirements (legal obligation).
  • To improve the site using aggregated analytics when you grant cookie consent (consent).

How long we keep your data

We retain personal data only for as long as it is needed:

  • General enquiries: up to 24 months after the last interaction, unless a continuing contract requires longer retention.
  • CVs and career expressions of interest: retained for 12 months so we can contact you about relevant opportunities, unless you ask us to remove them sooner.
  • Contractual project records: stored for the duration of the engagement plus the period needed to meet insurance or legal record keeping duties (typically 7 years).
  • Cookie consent preferences: stored in your browser for 12 months.

Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted processors who support our operations:

  • Cloudflare Pages Functions and Resend, which deliver form submissions securely to our inbox.
  • Google (Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics) when you grant analytics consent.
  • LinkedIn, Google Ads, or other marketing platforms only if you opt into marketing cookies.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, regulators, or law enforcement when required to comply with legal obligations.

International transfers

Our primary systems are hosted within the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. Where we use suppliers outside these regions, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, or an adequacy decision to protect your information.

Security

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted form submissions, role-based access to mailboxes, regular security updates for our infrastructure, and minimisation of the data we hold. We continuously review processors to ensure they meet equivalent security standards.

Your rights

Under EU and UK data protection laws you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your data or restrict its processing, in line with legal allowances.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain to your local supervisory authority if you have concerns about how we handle your data.

Contact us

To exercise your rights or ask a question about this notice, email privacy@dmarinternational.com or write to DMAR International MB, Litorinos g. 3, Kalotės k., Klaipėdos raj., 92275, Lithuania.
