Project Cargo and Heavy Transportation Assurance

Independent assurance to improve safety and insurability for high-value project cargo and heavy transport scopes.

Talk to an expert See case studies

Scope we review

  • Transport configuration review for loads, supports, and lashings
  • Motion and stability checks for route and season assumptions
  • Marine readiness reviews before vessel nomination and sail-away
  • Interfaces between engineering, marine spread, and offshore execution teams

Typical outputs

  • Independent comment matrix with clear close-out actions
  • Readiness status and hold-point recommendations
  • Go/no-go criteria linked to weather and vessel limitations
  • Concise reporting for developers, EPCIs, shipowners, and insurers
