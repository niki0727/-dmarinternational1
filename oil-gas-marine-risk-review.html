Oil & Gas Marine Risk Review

Our oil and gas marine assurance service provides independent marine risk review for offshore campaigns under schedule and cost pressure. We assess vessel readiness, method robustness, and execution interfaces so project teams can make better decisions before commitment and reduce the probability of expensive offshore disruption.

Talk to an expert See case studies

Timing of involvement

Clients involve us during brownfield campaigns, decommissioning-linked scopes, and transport-installation packages where changing conditions increase uncertainty.

Scope of review

  • Marine risk review oil and gas for campaign-critical operations
  • Offshore oil and gas vessel readiness checks
  • Oil and gas marine assurance challenge on methods and contingencies

Typical deliverables

  • Prioritised risk and readiness report
  • Action tracker with ownership and deadlines
  • Decision notes for project and marine leadership

Why oil and gas campaigns fail operationally

  • Uncontrolled risk migration into offshore execution
  • Late vessel or method changes with major cost impact
  • Inconsistent readiness standards across contractors

Relevant sectors

  • Brownfield and late-life field operations
  • Heavy transport and offshore construction
  • Decommissioning-related marine activities

Crisis-driven challenges

  • Operator / contractor marine governance interface
  • Vessel and asset interface during transfer and installation
  • Onshore risk assumptions versus offshore operating constraints

Standards and guidance considered

  • DNV and IMCA operational guidance
  • Client/operator assurance frameworks
  • Insurer and contractual technical requirements

FAQs

Is this suitable for late-life assets?
Yes. We regularly support campaigns where ageing infrastructure and tighter margins require stronger risk discipline.
Can you support urgent mobilisation windows?
Yes. We can start with focused critical-path review and expand as needed.
Do you work with operator assurance teams?
Yes. Our role is to complement internal assurance with independent challenge and practical outputs.
