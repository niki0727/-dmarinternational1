Sectors

We support developers, EPCIs and marine contractors across offshore energy, subsea, heavy-lift and industrial projects - providing technical assurance, engineering support and qualified personnel throughout the full project lifecycle.

Offshore Wind

Marine Warranty and engineering assurance for fixed-bottom and floating wind installation campaigns. Experience includes foundations, transition pieces, towers, nacelles, OSS modules, heavy-lift operations, port interface and vessel suitability across the North Sea, Baltic and Mediterranean.

Subsea Cables

Assurance for export and inter-array cable installation, nearshore landfalls, pull-ins, crossings and HDD interfaces. We support trenching, burial and marine spread readiness in complex shallow-water environments.

Oil & Gas / Decommissioning

Technical and MWS support for module transport, jacket installation, decommissioning lifts and towage. Activities include readiness checks, sea-fastening verification, transport engineering and compliance with DNV and IMCA standards.

Ports, Shipyards & Heavy-Lift

Quayside integration, SPMT load-outs, heavy transport preparations and rigging verification. We support fabrication yards and staging ports with engineering review, ballast plans, lifting checks and operational supervision.

Marine Civils & Nearshore

Support for nearshore marine civil works, including caissons, bridge elements, cofferdams and temporary works. We evaluate lifting methods, marine spreads and metocean windows close to shore.

Transportation & Project Cargo

Independent technical verification for heavy-lift and project cargo movements by barge, HLV or semi-submersible vessel. Services include grillage and seafastening review, stability and motions analysis, and vessel suitability surveys.

Manpower, Technicians & Project Execution Support

Provision of qualified offshore and onshore personnel for marine, wind and industrial projects. Our teams include wind turbine technicians (climbing-certified), welders and fabricators, riggers, deck crews and shipyard labour. We also support project management, site supervision and full execution assistance for offshore and onshore work packages.

Accredited Marine Warranty Surveyors (SOMWS-qualified or equivalent) and experienced marine consultants to support EPCIs, developers, and MWS companies during high-risk offshore operations.
