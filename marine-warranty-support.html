Marine Warranty Support for Offshore Projects

Our marine warranty support gives developers, EPCIs, shipowners, and MWS firms an independent marine warranty consultant view before costly commitments. We focus on practical close-out, evidence-led readiness, and faster approvals so offshore campaigns move from planning to execution with fewer surprises and stronger insurability.

Talk to an expert See case studies

When clients involve us

Clients involve us before vessel commitment, before final RAMS submission, and before weather-critical mobilisation windows where delays become expensive quickly.

What we review

  • Marine warranty surveyor style RAMS and procedure review
  • Marine warranty services checks on vessel suitability and marine spread readiness
  • Independent marine warranty consultant challenge on lifting, sea-fastening, and execution logic

Typical deliverables

  • Prioritised comment matrix with close-out owners and deadlines
  • Readiness summary for decision makers and insurers
  • Hold-point and go/no-go recommendation log

Key risks reduced

  • Late-stage technical rework after commercial lock-in
  • Aborts caused by unresolved method gaps
  • Insurer or client rejection due to weak evidence trails

Sectors supported

  • Offshore wind transport and installation
  • Subsea cable operations and landfalls
  • Oil and gas, decommissioning, and heavy-lift scopes

Common vessel / project interfaces

  • Developer / EPCI / MWS reviewer alignment
  • Marine spread and engineering team interface
  • Port, yard, and offshore execution interface

Standards and guidance considered

  • DNV-ST-N001 and related marine operation guidance
  • IMCA guidance for offshore operations and lifting
  • Client and contract-specific technical standards

FAQs

Do you replace a Lloyd's registered MWS company?
No. DMAR provides independent support and specialist surveyor expertise to help client and MWS workflows move faster and cleaner.
Can you support urgent campaigns?
Yes. We can start with focused critical-path review and build out full close-out tracking as documents mature.
Who receives your outputs?
Usually developers, EPCIs, MWS firms, shipowners, and insurers who need concise evidence for readiness decisions.
