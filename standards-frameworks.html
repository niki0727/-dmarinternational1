Standards and Frameworks We Work To

Our reviews reference widely used marine and offshore guidance to keep assurance outputs practical, traceable, and insurable.

Talk to an expert See case studies

Scope we review

  • DNV and class-related marine operation guidance where relevant
  • IMCA-aligned practices for offshore operations and lifting
  • Client and contract-specific standards integrated into review workflows
  • Interfaces between engineering, marine spread, and offshore execution teams

Typical outputs

  • Independent comment matrix with clear close-out actions
  • Readiness status and hold-point recommendations
  • Go/no-go criteria linked to weather and vessel limitations
  • Concise reporting for developers, EPCIs, shipowners, and insurers
