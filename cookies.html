Cookies and local storage

This notice explains how DMAR International MB uses cookies, pixels, and browser storage, and how you can control non-essential categories.

How we use cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies to deliver the site and keep the navigation secure. Optional analytics and marketing tags run only after you provide consent through the cookie banner.

Cookie categories

  • Essential: Required for site functionality such as remembering your cookie preferences. These cannot be switched off.
  • Analytics: Help us understand how the site is used so we can improve navigation and content.
  • Marketing: Enable third-party tags, such as Google Ads remarketing, when campaigns are active.
