Cookies & tracking technologies

This page explains which cookies and storage technologies are used on dmarinternational.com, why we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

How we categorise cookies

All cookies are grouped into the same categories you see in our banner:

  • Essential: Required for security, network management, and remembering your consent settings. These are always active because the site cannot operate correctly without them.
  • Analytics: Optional tags that help us understand how the site is used. We deploy Google Analytics via Google Tag Manager only after you give consent.
  • Marketing: Optional tags used for advertising and remarketing. After consent we may load Google Ads remarketing tags, the Meta Pixel, and the LinkedIn Insight Tag.

Third-party controls

If you wish, you can also manage cookies directly within your browser settings or install extensions that block analytics or advertising tags. Doing so may affect how our site functions or what content you see. We load Google Tag Manager and any analytics or marketing tags only after you have enabled the relevant categories.
